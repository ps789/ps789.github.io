Computer Science Ph.D. candidate · Georgia Tech

Phillip Si.

Learning from uncertainty.
Modeling the physical world.

I work on probabilistic machine learning, generative data assimilation, and differentiable surrogate models. My research brings sparse observations and dynamical models together to build efficient, uncertainty-aware digital twins.

At Georgia Tech, I’m advised by Peng Chen and Chao Zhang. I also conduct research at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, following work at Argonne National Laboratory.

What I work on

Making sense of complex systems.

From a few measurements to a fuller picture of how the world evolves.

01

Generative data assimilation

Combining score-based generative models and Bayesian filtering to infer high-dimensional states from sparse, noisy observations.

Latent-EnSF · LD-EnSF · LD-EnFF
02

Differentiable dynamics

Learning compact surrogate models for efficient forecasting, joint state–parameter estimation, and model predictive control.

LEVDA · Neural ODEs · Flow control
03

AI digital twins

Bringing simulation, observations, and learning together for flood prediction, weather, and unsteady fluid dynamics.

CLDNet · C-STRIDE · Flood forecasting
