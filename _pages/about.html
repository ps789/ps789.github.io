Phillip Si

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, advised by Peng Chen and Chao Zhang. I work on probabilistic machine learning and data assimilation for physical systems.

I’m also a graduate researcher at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, working on estimation and control for fluid flows. Previously, I worked on flood forecasting at Argonne National Laboratory.

Before Georgia Tech, I studied machine learning at Carnegie Mellon and Computer Science and Mathematics at Cornell, where I worked with Volodymyr Kuleshov.

Research

I’m interested in estimating the state of a physical system when only a small part of it can be observed. I develop methods that combine generative models with learned dynamics to make forecasting and data assimilation faster.

Recent projects include LEVDA for joint state and parameter estimation, LD-EnSF for filtering with sparse observations, and CLDNet for high-resolution flood prediction.
